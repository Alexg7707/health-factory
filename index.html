In the kitchen

One menu, four ideas.

A day at the table

Borrow a dish or two and swap in whatever looks good at the market.

Breakfast
Simmer oats with apple and cinnamon, then finish with a spoonful of yogurt.
Lunch
Toss bulgur with chickpeas, roasted peppers, feta, herbs and a squeeze of lemon.
Dinner
Roast potatoes until crisp. Serve with fish or white beans and some shredded cabbage.
Snack
Spread cream cheese on toast and top with sliced pear. Add cinnamon if you like.
