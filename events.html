Events · Winter 2026–27

A midnight run and two winter bird counts

You can spend a day with local birders, run through Central Park as the year turns, or give a February morning fifteen minutes by the window.

Dates checked November 21, 2026

On the calendar

Dec 14–Jan 5 · Christmas Bird Count Dec 31 · NYRR Midnight Run Feb 12–15 · Great Backyard Bird Count

Dec 14, 2026 – Jan 5, 2027 · Americas and Pacific

Christmas Bird Count

Each volunteer group counts the birds in the same 15-mile-wide circle for one day. Enter your town in Audubon’s Circle Finder to see which day your group will go out and contact its leader before you join. If you’re learning to tell birds apart, ask to go with an experienced counter. If your feeder falls inside the circle, ask whether you can count from home.

Find your local count and sign up · Read Audubon’s participation guide

Dec 31, 2026 · New York City

NYRR Midnight Run

In Manhattan, the New Year’s Eve countdown starts at 11:59 p.m. local time. At midnight, runners set off on a four-mile race through Central Park. New York Road Runners lists entries for runners ages eight and up. Check the race page for an available entry and its fee before you make the trip.

Check race details and registration with NYRR

Feb 12–15, 2027 · Worldwide

Great Backyard Bird Count

A park bench or an apartment window will do. Choose one spot and watch or listen for at least 15 minutes on any of the four days. First-time birders can use Merlin Bird ID to identify and save a sighting. If you already keep a list in eBird, submit it as usual. Sightings shared through either service during the count go toward the event automatically.

Follow the organizers’ guide to reporting a sighting
